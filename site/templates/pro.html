Rxcurve Pro: tell us what would help

Rxcurve stays free: every payment limit, chart and page you see today. Rxcurve Pro would add tools for people who work with Part B drug prices every quarter. Nothing is for sale yet; the waitlist decides what gets built first.

Being considered

  • Alerts for your codes the day CMS posts a new or revised file, with each code’s new payment limit and change.
  • Excel and CSV exports of your codes, a drug class, or every change between two quarters.
  • Saved NCR scenarios: keep your own doses, contract prices and product mix in the NCR calculator.
  • Full history for any set of codes, with every revised CMS file side by side.
  • Company views: Medicare Part B payment limits and spending grouped by parent company and stock ticker.

Pro will not change what is free, and it will never include advertising, sponsored placements or paid rankings. Every figure will still come straight from the CMS files.
