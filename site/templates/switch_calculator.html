Biosimilar switching calculator

What would Medicare and its patients spend if some of a reference product’s use moved to a biosimilar?

  1. Pick a market and the biosimilar that gains the units.
  2. Choose how much moves: the percent of the reference product’s {{ units_year }} Medicare units that switch.
  3. Read the result: the change in Medicare’s allowed amount, split into what Medicare pays and what patients pay.

Units are each product’s {{ units_year }} Medicare Part B units (the newest year CMS has published with units). Prices are the CMS {{ latest_q|quarter }} payment limits and coinsurance percentages. This is a what-if at today’s prices, not a forecast. It is not billing, coding or financial advice.

How the numbers are calculated
Units after (reference) = {{ units_year }} units × (1 − switch %) Units after (biosimilar) = {{ units_year }} units + reference units × switch % Allowed amount = units × payment limit ({{ latest_q|quarter }}) Patient coinsurance = allowed amount × coinsurance % Medicare pays = allowed amount − patient coinsurance

Units. Each product’s total billing units from CMS’s Medicare Part B Spending by Drug file for {{ units_year }} ({% for src in units_sources %}{% if src.url %}{{ src.name }}{% else %}{{ src.name }}{% endif %}{{ ", " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}). Only products billed in the same unit as the reference product, in {{ units_year }} and in {{ latest_q|quarter }}, are included, so units move one for one.

Prices. Payment limits and coinsurance percentages are from the CMS {{ latest_q|quarter }} payment limit file. Payment limits change every quarter and reflect sales from about two quarters earlier.

Not modeled. The Part B deductible, the 2% sequestration cut, supplemental insurance that pays a patient’s coinsurance, Medicare Advantage, growth in use since {{ units_year }}, and future price changes. Which products compete comes from the site’s biosimilar groups list, checked by hand; it is not a CMS file.
