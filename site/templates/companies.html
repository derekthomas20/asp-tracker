Mostly shared with other makers

For these {{ mostly|length }} companies, most Medicare spending on their drugs is on codes other companies also make, often generic or multi-source drugs. CMS reports spending per code, not per company, so that spending can’t be split between makers, and the spending on their own codes is the smaller part of the Medicare spending on their drugs. These companies are not comparable with those above. “Shared codes” is Medicare spending on every code the company shares, all makers together; the same code is counted for each of its makers.
