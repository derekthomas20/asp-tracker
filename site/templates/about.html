About this data

Rxcurve reports Medicare Part B drug payment limits, quarter by quarter, straight from the CMS files.

Where it comes from

Every number on Rxcurve is computed by a script from the quarterly Medicare Part B drug payment limit files that the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) publishes on its ASP pricing files page. The site covers {{ first_q|quarter }} to {{ latest_q|quarter }}. When CMS re-posts a corrected file for a past quarter, the newer file is used and the earlier one is kept and shown as an earlier version.

What a payment limit is

For most Part B drugs, the payment limit is based on the Average Sales Price (ASP) that manufacturers report to CMS. Each quarter's limit reflects sales data from about two quarters earlier. For example, the {{ latest_q|quarter }} limits reflect sales from around {{ sales_q|quarter }} ({{ sales_q|months }}). So a change in a payment limit shows up months after the change in prices that caused it.

What it does not mean

  • Inclusion in the file does not mean Medicare covers a drug. Coverage depends on Medicare rules and coverage decisions, not on this list.
  • The payment limit is not what a patient pays or what a provider is guaranteed to receive.
  • Where CMS gives no payment limit for a code, this site shows N/A.

Independence

Rxcurve is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to CMS or any other government agency. Check the CMS files before relying on any figure.
