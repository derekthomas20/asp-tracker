How the numbers are calculated
Billing units / package = package strength (mg) ÷ HCPCS unit (mg) Net cost / unit = contract price × (1 − rebate %) ÷ billing units Paid / unit = payment limit × (1 + limit change) × [Medicare share × (1 − 0.8 × 2% if sequestration) + commercial share × commercial %] NCR / unit = paid / unit − net cost / unit NCR / dose = paid / unit × billed units − acquisition cost of the vials used

Payment limit. Enter the per-unit payment limit from the CMS quarterly ASP file. For a reference product it is ASP + 6%. For a qualifying biosimilar it is the biosimilar's ASP plus 8% of the reference product's ASP, so a biosimilar can pay more per unit than its own price suggests. Confirm each product's add-on period. Limits reflect sales from about two quarters earlier, so use the limit-change slider to test erosion.

Doses. Doses round up to whole billing units. Without leftover reuse, multi-dose vials are drawn first so any waste falls on single-dose vials, where JW billing can apply. With reuse, single-dose vials are drawn first and the multi-dose remainder is saved, so only the drug actually used is costed. Vial selection minimizes discarded drug rather than maximizing billable waste.

Not modeled. Patient coinsurance collection, manufacturer discarded-drug refunds, 340B pricing, administration fees, and payer step or preferred-product rules. A payer that requires one product makes NCR moot for those patients.
